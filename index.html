Chao Liu

Research Scientist, NVIDIA Research

About

I am a Research Scientist at NVIDIA Research, where I work on generative models for video and 3D, with a focus on world models and Physical AI. My research spans video generation, fast generative sampling, controllable and 3D-consistent generation, and physics-based computational imaging.

I received my Ph.D. in Robotics from Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Srinivasa G. Narasimhan and Artur W. Dubrawski.

Research Interests

  • Video generation and world models: long-horizon, autoregressive, and bidirectional video diffusion, and temporally consistent noise for equivariant video models, as a foundation for simulating the physical world.
  • Fast generative sampling: distillation methods that bring diffusion and flow models down to a few network evaluations without losing diversity.
  • Controllable and compositional generation: structured representations such as blobs and camera poses that give users precise control over images, videos, and 3D scenes.
  • 3D vision and generation: 3D-consistent synthesis, point cloud generation, and real-time 3D portrait reconstruction for telepresence.
  • Computational imaging and physics-based vision: recovering depth, shape, thin structures, and subsurface tissue from compact camera and lighting setups such as focal stacks, near-light photometric stereo, and diffuse optical tomography, as well as diffusion-based MRI reconstruction.
